## 3. What does your project do? (short description, ~2–3 sentences)

> The STEM in Me helps underserved families in Los Angeles navigate the LAUSD
> magnet school system and get their kids into STEM programs. We built STEM
> Pathfinder, a free web tool that lets parents search, compare, and get
> matched to all 180 LAUSD magnet schools — scored on program quality,
> enrollment access, and equity — and we run in-person application workshops
> during the November–February magnet application window.

---

## 4. Longer description / tell us more (if asked)

> The LAUSD magnet lottery is one of the best paths into strong STEM programs
> for kids in South LA, East LA, and Watts — but the system is opaque, the
> deadlines are unforgiving, and the families who would benefit most are the
> least likely to have help navigating it.
>
> We built STEM Pathfinder, a free, no-login school finder covering all 180
> LAUSD magnet schools. Parents answer three questions and get ranked matches with plain-
> language explanations, an interactive map, and direct links to apply. The
> site works offline-first, is built for phones, and includes a built-in
> helper chat that answers application questions without collecting any data.
>
> Our next step is in-person: free application workshops at libraries and
> community centers during the application window (applications open in
> November), where volunteers sit with families and help them complete their
> magnet applications using the tool. Our goal for the first season is to help
> 25 families in South LA complete applications.
>
> Fiscal sponsorship would let us accept donations to cover workshop costs
> (printing, space, outreach flyers in English and Spanish) and a proper
> domain name.
