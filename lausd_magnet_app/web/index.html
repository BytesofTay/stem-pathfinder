About The STEM in Me

Choosing a magnet program takes more than finding a name on a list. Families need clear program information, accurate grade ranges, and a practical next step.

The STEM in Me created STEM Pathfinder to help Los Angeles families explore magnet schools, compare options, and find official application information. No account is needed. Our directory comes from CDE; program descriptions are reviewed separately and linked to their sources.

How to read this directory

🎓
Directory facts Grades, addresses, websites and school identifiers come from the dated CDE directory. A school can have multiple programs with different requirements.
🚪
Reviewed program details Reviewed interests link to an official school page and a checked date. They describe the published program, not school quality or guaranteed availability.
⚖️
Still to check “Program details not yet reviewed” means we have not added a source for this school. It does not mean the school lacks a STEM program. Confirm entry grades, eligibility and transport with the school.
